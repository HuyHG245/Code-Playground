Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This privacy policy describes how Code Playground (the "service"), hosted on GitHub Pages, collects and handles data.

1. Information we collect

You can use Code Playground without an account. If you choose to sign in using Google, Facebook, or GitHub, we receive only the basic profile information your chosen provider returns for login: your name and email address (and your account's unique identifier). We do not store your password.

When signed in and you save scripts, we store the script content you provide, its title, the programming language, and a timestamp.

2. How we use your information

  • To keep you signed in and identify your saved scripts.
  • To store, list, load, and delete the scripts you save.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.

3. Third-party services

The service uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore (Google LLC) to handle sign-in and to store your saved scripts, and your login flows through the third party you choose: Google LLC (Google), Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook), or GitHub, Inc. These providers' own privacy policies apply to the initial account access.

4. Local storage

The service stores editor settings and a local copy of your last script in your browser's localStorage. This stays on your device and is never transmitted to the server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You may delete any of your saved scripts at any time from the saved scripts list in the app.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us (see below).

6. Data retention

Saved scripts and account data are kept until you delete them or request deletion.

7. Children's privacy

The service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or data deletion requests: youremail@example.com
